Reclaim your empire. One cut at a time.

Your empire is there for the taking. Draw a line through hostile space. Make it back to safe territory. Close the loop and claim a piece of it.

Galaxtix is an arcade territory-capture roguelite where every cut is a gamble. Reach farther to claim more, but leave your trail exposed for longer.

CUT. CLOSE. CONQUER.
Turn enemy territory into safe ground. Trap enemies, dodge beams, and thread your way past moving hazards. A clever cut can change the whole fight.

BUILD YOUR COMEBACK
Choose from three ships with different capture mechanics. Draft abilities during a run, improve your systems, and spend salvage on permanent upgrades. Dash through danger, shield a risky cut, or charge a capture disc.

TAKE BACK THREE ACTS
Push through the Foundry, the Infestation, and the Reactor. Each brings distinct territory patterns, layouts, enemies, and a pool of bosses. Race an opponent, outclaim a rival before time runs out, or bring cargo home.

CARVE DOWN THE BOSSES
Dismantle defenses to expose a protected core. Dodge the Prism Warden's split beams. Catch the Thorn Maw while it's vulnerable and cut pieces out of its body.
