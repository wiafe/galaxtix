# Steam store direction · 10 September 2026

## Copy

Lead with the player's goal, name the genre immediately, and explain the physical action. Keep the short description plain text and move the feature detail below the screenshots. This follows [Steam's description guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/page/description).

The official store pitches for [Nova Drift](https://store.steampowered.com/app/858210/Nova_Drift/) and [Dome Keeper](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1637320/Dome_Keeper/) connect their genre to concrete actions, builds, and risk. Our editorial takeaway: sell Galaxtix through cutting, closing loops, and exposing your trail. These examples inform the writing; they do not establish conversion results.

The empire framing supplies motivation without promising empire management or a story campaign. All feature claims refer to the current roguelite implementation. Other game modes are intentionally outside this asset set's scope.

## Screenshots

Steam asks for at least five screenshots, recommends high-resolution widescreen images, and expects actual gameplay. Menu images should show a distinctive component of the game. No captions, artwork, or promotional text have been added to these images.
