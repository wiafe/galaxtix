# Trailer rough cut v3

The closing GALAXTIX title now uses filled, angular lettering inspired by the approved patterned capsule (`capsule-f-rival-frontier-v4.png`). The broad strokes, clipped G corners, triangular A counters, and white/cyan treatment replace the thin outline title.

The wordmark is editable geometry in `scripts/galaxtix_wordmark.gd` (originally `tools/trailer_wordmark.gd`), rendered through the game's CRT pipeline. End-card bloom is reduced to keep the solid letters readable. The tagline remains in the game's stroke lettering.

The edit remains 54.2 seconds with the same gameplay sequence and temporary soundtrack as v2. The latest movie is `galaxtix-roguelite-trailer-rough-v3.mp4`; `rough-v3-poster.png` previews the new ending. The builder now uses `endcard-filled.avi` when available and writes v3 deliverables. Earlier versions are retained.
